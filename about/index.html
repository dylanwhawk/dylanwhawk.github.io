About

I have experience building agentic pipelines that scale the creation of RL environments and evaluations for LLMs. I'm now working on ways to measure and improve the quality of LLM training data, especially RL environments. I think that both studying how data shapes the learning signal, and improving the pipelines that produce it, can do a lot for model alignment. Most of what I write here comes out of that work, and I'd like to hear from you if it overlaps with yours.
